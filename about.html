About VoxAI

Built by creators, for creators.

Our mission

At VoxAI, we believe every creator deserves a professional voice — without spending hundreds on studio time or voice actors. Our mission is to make studio-quality AI voices accessible, affordable, and global.

What we do

VoxAI is a text-to-speech platform powered by neural networks. We turn written text into lifelike audio in 30+ languages and dozens of voices, optimized for:

  • YouTube creators
  • TikTok and short-form video
  • Podcasters
  • Educators and e-learning creators
  • Developers building voice-enabled apps

Why creators choose us

We focus on three things: quality, speed, and fairness. That means a generous free plan, no hidden fees, full commercial rights on paid plans, and voices that genuinely sound human.

The team

We're a small, focused team of audio engineers, ML researchers, and designers building the voice tools we wish existed. We're remote-first and serve creators in over 100 countries.
