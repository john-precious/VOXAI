Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VoxAI ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, password (encrypted).
  • Usage data: text you submit, voices used, generation history.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device, cookies.
  • Payment data: processed securely by Stripe (we do not store card details).

2. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the VoxAI service.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To send service-related and (with consent) marketing emails.
  • To prevent fraud and enforce our Terms of Service.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use cookies to remember preferences and analyze traffic. We partner with advertising networks such as Google AdSense and rewarded ad providers to display ads. These partners may use cookies to personalize ads. You can opt out via your browser settings or AboutAds.info.

4. AI-generated content

Audio you generate may be stored temporarily on our servers to deliver it to you. We do not use your text or audio to train our voice models without consent.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with service providers (hosting, payments, analytics) under strict data agreements, or when legally required.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing privacy@voxai.example. GDPR and CCPA rights are respected.

7. Children

VoxAI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify users of significant changes via email or on this page.

9. Contact

Questions? Email privacy@voxai.example.
